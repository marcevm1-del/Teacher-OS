2. **Salary jump from the Dubai post.** This is the biggest money lever in the whole plan. Dubai applications are the most important work this quarter.
3. **Private tutoring** (fast cash, high rate per hour).
4. **Teaching materials** (slow to start, builds up over time, pays while you sleep).
5. Apps and photography: these come in 2028, as the Route already says. Don't expect them to pay the debt.

## 7. Things that will break the plan (and what to do)

- **Moving costs eat the debt money in July/August.** Ask Dubai schools about relocation allowances and housing when you negotiate. Save for the move separately from April onwards.
- **Four big goals all land in 2027** (Dubai move, debt, 72 kg, DM Pro). Your Route already puts lili off to 2028. If something else has to give, delay DM Pro, not the debt or Dubai.
- **Side income starts slowly.** A new TPT shop often takes months to get regular sales. Tutoring is what pays the debt in 2026–27. TPT pays off later.
- **"Rich" won't happen in one year.** The Five-Year Route is how you get there. The quick win is being debt-free by August 2027, and this plan is built to get you there.
