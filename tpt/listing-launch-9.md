## Description (paste as is)

Summary writing looks simple and loses students marks every year: copying phrases, including examples, going over 120 words. And from 2027 there's a brand-new 2(b) question on the writer's attitudes. This workbook teaches both, one clear step at a time.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The 5-step summary method: lock the focus, harvest, group, write once, check
• Own-words toolkit: phrase swaps and umbrella words, with examples
• "Five mistakes that cost summary marks": weak and strong versions side by side
• 6 original articles (fast fashion, boredom, life at a polar research station, cycling cities, volunteering abroad, protecting old buildings)
• Articles 1–3: guided, with a planning page (focus heading, points-harvest table, grouping boxes)
• Articles 4–6: independent, exam-style, with answer space
• Every article has a 2(a) summary question and a 2(b) attitudes question
• Answers for every article: a list of creditable points, a model summary (101–109 words) and a model 2(b) answer
• Marking bands and a progress tracker

PAIRS WITH
The Language Analysis Workbook (Q3), the Paper 1 Practice Packs (six full papers) and the Complete Exam Guide. Everything is in the 0500 Complete Bundle.

Independent practice material; not produced or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment. All articles are original. Licensed for use by one teacher/classroom (including a copy for each student) or one student.
