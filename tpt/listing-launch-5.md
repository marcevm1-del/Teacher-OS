## Description (paste as is)

32 printable revision cards for the NEW 2027 Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) exam. Every method, form, technique and timing plan students need, colour-coded and ready to cut out.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 10 Paper 1 cards: every question from Q1 to Q4, including the new 2(b) attitudes question and 2-mark word meanings
• 7 Paper 2 Section A cards: 1(a), the five-step method for 1(b), the "repeat → evaluate" ladder, questioning evidence, and the four forms
• 4 composition cards: descriptive and narrative structures, dos and don'ts
• 5 language cards: imagery, sound, structure, persuasive devices and phrases for explaining effect
• 6 exam technique cards: command words, timing plans for both papers, and the top 10 mistakes
• A "how to revise with them" page

Print on card, cut along the dashed lines, and revise anywhere. Teachers may print a set for every student in their class.

PAIRS WITH
The Complete Exam Guide and the practice packs. The cards give students the method, and the packs give them the practice. All five are available together in the 0500 Complete Bundle.

Independent revision material; not produced or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment. Licensed for use by one teacher/classroom or one student.
