Products only earn once people find them. This plan gets all fifteen listings live in one week, then drives traffic for four weeks. The time needed is about 20–30 minutes a day.

TPT tips:
- **The first 13 words of the title matter most** for search. Every title here starts with "IGCSE English 0500", which is exactly what teachers type.
- **Put "0500" and "2027" in every title.** These are the words that tell a buyer your product is up to date.
- **Link products to each other** in each description ("Pairs with…") and add every product to the bundle.
- **Reply to every review and question within 24 hours.** It shows up on your store page.
- **Join TPT's sitewide sales** when TPT announces them (usually around back-to-school and in late November). Buyers wait for these.

## Where your buyers are
1. **Teacher Facebook groups:** international school English teachers, IGCSE/Cambridge teachers, UAE/GCC teacher groups. Read each group's rules first. Many allow free resources any day but paid promotion only on set days.
2. **LinkedIn:** Heads of English and school leaders. Good for the free briefing and for Department Licences.
3. **Pinterest:** teachers search Pinterest for resources, and pins keep bringing people to TPT for months. Pin every preview image with a link to its listing.
4. **Parents** (for tutoring): the adverts are in `tutoring/tutoring-kit.md`.

> The hardest shift in the new 0500 Paper 2: students now have to **evaluate** the texts, not just repeat them. Here's the ladder I use with my students:

> "The text says visitor numbers fell by 40%" → "However, opening hours were halved, so the fall may reflect closed doors, not lost interest."
> My Paper 2 pack has four full tasks (speech, letter, article, report) with annotated model answers that show exactly this: [link]

## Weekly routine (Sunday, 30 minutes)
- [ ] Check TPT stats: views, sales, followers. Which product gets views but no sales? Improve its thumbnail or first line.
- [ ] Post that week's message in 3–5 groups + LinkedIn.
- [ ] Pin 4 images.
- [ ] Send 10 school emails (`money/school-sales-kit.md`).
- [ ] Log any income in the tracker and put it toward the debt.
