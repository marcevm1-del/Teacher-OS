## Description (paste as is)

Four complete Paper 2 Section A tasks written for the NEW 2027 Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) exam, each with a mark scheme and an annotated top-band model answer.

From June 2027, directed writing is split into Question 1(a), a new 5-mark structured question on how writers influence readers, and Question 1(b), a 35-mark speech, letter, article or report. For the first time, Paper 2 asks students to evaluate the texts, not just use them. This pack teaches exactly that.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 4 complete Section A tasks (40 marks each), one in each form: speech, letter, article and report
• 6 original source texts (550–600 words per task) on topics students will want to argue about: phones in schools, entrance fees for beauty spots, compulsory school sport, and the future of a town library
• Texts deliberately include evidence students can evaluate: unsourced statistics, single anecdotes, small surveys and one-sided claims
• Question 1(a) analysis and evaluation questions with mark schemes
• Question 1(b) tasks with indicative content, including a list of "evaluation opportunities" for each task
• 4 annotated model answers (285–325 words), each with "Why this scores highly" notes
• Form toolkit: the conventions of speeches, letters, articles and reports
• "From repeating to evaluating" ladder and an evaluation phrase bank
• A photocopiable 1(b) planning frame
• Marking bands for Reading (10) and Writing (25), plus a score tracker

PERFECT FOR
Teaching directed writing form by form, timed practice, mocks, homework, tutoring and independent revision for students sitting 0500 in 2027, 2028 or 2029.

PAIRS WITH
The IGCSE 0500 Complete Exam Guide and the Paper 1 Practice Pack. All three are available together in the 0500 Complete Bundle.

WRITTEN BY
A PGCE-qualified (Summa Cum Laude) English teacher with experience teaching Cambridge IGCSE English in UAE international schools. All texts are original.

PLEASE NOTE
This is independent practice material based on the published 2027–2029 syllabus. It is not produced or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment. The marking bands are written for this pack and are not official Cambridge descriptors. Licensed for use by one teacher/classroom or one student.
