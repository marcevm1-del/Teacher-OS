## Description (paste as is)

The Paper 1 Question 3 language task is where many students lose the most marks. They spot techniques, write "this creates vivid imagery", and never explain the effect. This workbook fixes that with a clear method, lots of practice and a model answer for every extract.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The Choose → Zoom → Connect method, with a bank of phrases for explaining effect
• "Six mistakes that cost marks": weak and strong versions side by side
• 6 original extracts written for this workbook (a storm, a wildfire, a corner shop, a sandstorm, a penalty kick, a hospital waiting room)
• Extracts 1–3: guided practice with a Choose → Zoom → Connect table and a worked example
• Extracts 4–6: independent, exam-style practice (200–250 words)
• 6 model answers (204–216 words), each covering both paragraphs and ending with an overall effect
• Marking bands and a progress tracker

PERFECT FOR
Teaching writer's effects, intervention groups, homework, tutoring and independent revision for the 2027 Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) exam. The method works for any language analysis question.

PAIRS WITH
The Paper 1 Practice Packs (six full papers, each with a language task) and the Complete Exam Guide. Everything is in the 0500 Complete Bundle.

Independent practice material; not produced or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment. All extracts are original. Licensed for use by one teacher/classroom (including a copy for each student) or one student.
