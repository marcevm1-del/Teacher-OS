## Description (paste as is)

Updated for the NEW 2027 Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) syllabus.

The 0500 exam was restructured for June 2027 onwards. Paper 1 now has four 20-mark questions, the summary task has a new part 2(b), word-meaning questions are worth 2 marks, and Paper 2's directed writing is now split into 1(a) and 1(b). Most older revision guides and many past papers no longer match what students will face. This guide does.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The exam at a glance: both papers, every question, marks and word limits
• What changed for 2027, in plain language
• Paper 1 Q1 Comprehension: the Find → Prove → Phrase framework
• Paper 1 Q2 Summary: a 5-step method for 120-word summaries, plus the new 2(b) attitudes question
• Paper 1 Q3: word meanings in context and the 200–250 word language task (Choose → Zoom → Connect)
• Paper 1 Q4 Extended response: form conventions for all six text types
• Paper 2 Section A: directed writing 1(a) and 1(b), with how to evaluate texts, not just repeat them
• Paper 2 Section B: descriptive vs narrative composition, with structures for each
• Weak vs strong model answers with commentary
• Command words decoded
• Minute-by-minute timing plans for both papers
• Top 10 mark-losing mistakes and how to fix them
• Exam-week revision checklist

WHO IT'S FOR
Students preparing for Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) exams in 2027, 2028 or 2029. Ideal for independent revision, homework, intervention groups, tutoring, and as a class handout at the start of Year 10 or 11.

WRITTEN BY
A PGCE-qualified (Summa Cum Laude) English teacher with experience teaching Cambridge IGCSE English in UAE international schools. All example texts are original.

PLEASE NOTE
This is an independent study guide based on the published 2027–2029 syllabus. It is not produced or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment. Licensed for use by one teacher/classroom or one student.
