## Description (paste as is)

Everything students need for the composition task, Paper 2 Section B of the Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) exam, updated for 2027. Candidates choose one of four titles (two descriptive, two narrative) and write 350–450 words. Every mark is for writing. This pack shows students exactly how to earn those marks.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Descriptive writing toolkit: a five-part structure (wide view → zoom → movement → shift → return), techniques that lift a description, and the traps that pull marks down
• Narrative writing toolkit: the story mountain, four ways to open, craft essentials and what to avoid
• 20 exam-style titles, arranged in 5 practice sets of 4 (2 descriptive + 2 narrative), just like the exam
• 2 photocopiable planning sheets (descriptive and narrative)
• Sentence upgrade workshop: 8 flat sentences to rewrite, with model upgrades and the technique each one shows
• 4 annotated model compositions (361–383 words): 2 descriptive, 2 narrative, each with "What makes it work" notes
• Marking bands for content & structure and style & accuracy
• Student self-assessment checklist and score tracker
• A suggested four-lesson teaching sequence

PERFECT FOR
Creative writing units, timed practice, homework, tutoring and independent revision for students sitting 0500 in 2027, 2028 or 2029. Works well with other creative writing courses too.
