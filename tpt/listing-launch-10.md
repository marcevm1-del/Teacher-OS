## Description (paste as is)

Question 4 is worth 20 marks, and the most common mistake is simply retelling the text with a greeting on top. This workbook teaches students to select, develop and write in role, in every form the exam can ask for.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The Mine → Stretch → Voice method and a "retelling → developing" ladder
• Conventions for all six forms: journal, report, interview, speech, letter and article
• "Five mistakes that cost Q4 marks": weak and strong versions side by side
• 6 original passages, one for each form (a lighthouse keeper's last night, a school garden, a mountain rescue, a market fire, a Japanese exchange, a robotics competition)
• Passages 1–3: guided, with a three-column planning page
• Passages 4–6: independent, exam-style, with answer space
• Answers for every passage: indicative content for each bullet, a model answer (271–298 words) and "Why it scores highly" notes
• Marking bands (10 reading + 10 writing) and a progress tracker

PAIRS WITH
The Summary Writing Workbook (Q2) and the Language Analysis Workbook (Q3). Together they form the Paper 1 Skills Bundle.

Independent practice material; not produced or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment. All passages are original. Licensed for use by one teacher/classroom (including a copy for each student) or one student.
