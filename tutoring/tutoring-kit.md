Goal: **4 paid hours a week at ~200 AED = ~3,200 AED/month** (this alone covers the full 3,000 AED debt payment).

Before the first paid lesson: MOHRE private-teacher work permit (free) plus a no-objection letter from your school. Never tutor students you teach at school.

## 3. Free consultation → paid package (20 min)

1. **Ask (5 min):** Which paper worries them most? Current grade vs target? When is the exam?
2. **Diagnose (10 min):** Give a 2-mark implicit question and one word-meaning question from the guide. Mark it live and show exactly where the marks went.
3. **Close (5 min):** *"In 8 lessons we'd cover every question type with a method for each. The term package is 1,600 AED and includes the guide. Shall I send you the first available slots?"* Then stop talking.
