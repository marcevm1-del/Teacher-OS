**How to pay it:** make the minimum payment on everything, and put every extra dirham on the debt with the **highest interest rate** first ("avalanche"). If one balance is small, clearing it first for a quick win ("snowball") is fine too. Just don't let a high-interest card sit there.

If any part is on a credit card, call the bank and ask for (a) a fixed-rate instalment plan or (b) a balance transfer to a lower rate. One phone call can save hundreds of dirhams a month.

## 3. Side-income engine (ranked by how fast it pays)

### A. Private IGCSE English tutoring: fastest money
- **Legal first:** get the MOHRE private-teacher work permit (free, lasts 2 years) plus a no-objection letter from your employer. Check your school contract doesn't ban private tutoring of your own students. Never tutor your own students.
- **Rate:** check what local tutors charge on tutoring sites and Facebook groups. IGCSE English commonly goes for roughly 150–250 AED/hour in the UAE, and online lessons let you teach students in any emirate.
- **Target:** 4 hours a week × ~200 AED ≈ **3,200 AED/month**. That alone pays the whole debt instalment.
- **Your edge:** you wrote the guide for the new 2027 syllabus. Give every student a copy. It shows you know the new exam, which most tutors don't yet.
- **Exam-season group classes (Apr–May 2027):** a 4-session "Paper 1 & 2 Bootcamp" with 6 students at 150 AED/session ≈ 3,600 AED per group.

### B. Teaching materials (TPT + selling direct): builds up slowly
See [`../tpt/product-pipeline.md`](../tpt/product-pipeline.md). Timing matters: the new 0500 syllabus starts in June 2027, so right now there are very few up-to-date guides for it. Get products listed before other sellers catch up.

**Honest numbers:** on TPT's free Basic seller plan you keep 55% minus a transaction fee. On the paid Premium plan you keep 80%. Check TPT's current seller fee page before choosing. At $12.99 on Premium you get roughly $10 (~37 AED) per sale. 30 sales a month across all products ≈ 1,100 AED. That's a good extra, but it builds over months. Don't count on it for the first debt payments.
