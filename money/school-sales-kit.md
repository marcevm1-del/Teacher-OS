# Selling direct to schools: Department Licences

**Why:** one Department Licence at 750 AED earns roughly what 20 TPT sales do, and a school can pay it from its resources budget. There are many British-curriculum schools in the UAE teaching IGCSE English, and every one of them has to prepare for the new 0500 exam.

**Target:** 1 licence a month = 750 AED a month toward the debt. That's about a quarter of the monthly payment from one email campaign.

Attach: [`PassWithPurpose-Department-Licence-Offer.pdf`](PassWithPurpose-Department-Licence-Offer.pdf). First put your email and WhatsApp in `tpt/products/department-offer/build.py` and rebuild.

---

## Step 0: check before you sell (this week)
- [ ] **Your employment contract.** Many schools restrict outside work, especially selling to other schools. If needed, ask HR in writing for permission to sell your own teaching resources.
- [ ] **Invoicing as an individual.** Selling to UAE schools and issuing invoices may need a licence (for example a freelance permit or trade licence, depending on your emirate). Check with your free zone or economic department before your first invoice. TPT sales go through TPT, so this applies only to direct sales.
- [ ] **Never sell to, or through, your current school's students**, and don't use school email or time for this business.

## Step 1: build the list (1 evening)
- Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Northern Emirates school directories (KHDA, ADEK and SPEA publish lists of private schools and their curricula) → filter for **British / IGCSE**.
- For each school: name, emirate, Head of English/Secondary English Lead (from the school website or LinkedIn), email.
- Keep a simple sheet: `School | Contact | Email | Sent | Follow-up | Reply | Result`.
- Aim for **40 schools** in the first list.

## Step 2: the first email (send 10 a week, personalised)

**Subject:** New 0500 practice papers for your Year 10–11 team

> Dear [Name],
>
> I'm an English teacher in the UAE, and I've written a set of practice resources for the new 2027 Cambridge IGCSE First Language English (0500) exam: three full Paper 1 practice papers in the new four-question format, four Paper 2 directed writing tasks with the new 1(a) question, a composition pack and a complete exam guide. All texts are original, and every task has a full mark scheme.
>
> As past papers from earlier years no longer match the new format, I thought they might save your team some time before mocks. I've attached a short overview.
>
> I'd be happy to send you a **free sample** (a full Paper 1 practice set and a 2027 changes briefing for your department) so your team can judge the quality for themselves.
>
> Kind regards,
> Marcé van Moerkerken
> PGCE Secondary English (Summa Cum Laude)

## Step 3: follow-up (7 days later, same thread)

> Dear [Name], just a quick follow-up in case this got buried. Would a free sample be useful for your department? Happy to send it today.

## Step 4: when they say yes to the sample
- Send: `FREE-IGCSE-0500-2027-Changes-Teacher-Briefing.pdf` + **Set 1 only** of the Paper 1 pack (to make it, copy pages 1–11 of the pack into a new PDF).
- Then, 5 days later:

> Dear [Name], I hope the sample was useful. If your team would like the full set before mocks, the Department Licence is 750 AED for every English teacher in the school, with unlimited printing for students. Shall I send an invoice to your finance team?

## Step 5: invoice and deliver
- Invoice: your name/licence details, school name and address, "PassWithPurpose 0500 Department Licence (one site)", amount, bank details, due in 30 days.
- On payment: email all PDFs, plus a short licence note: *"This confirms that [School] holds a PassWithPurpose 0500 Department Licence for one school site, [date]. Resources may be printed for students of the school and may not be shared outside it."*

## Step 6: turn buyers into more money
- **CPD upsell:** offer the 60-minute "Teaching the 2027 changes" online session at the licence + CPD price (1,250 AED). Base the session on the free briefing.
- **Referrals:** *"If colleagues at other schools in your group would find this useful, I'd be grateful for an introduction."*
- **Next year:** when you add new practice papers, offer existing schools an upgrade.

## Pricing notes
- 750 AED sits below what most departments can approve on their own. If schools say yes instantly, raise it to 950.
- Groups of schools: offer 20% off each additional school.
- TPT's own school licences (sold as "additional licences") are separate. This direct offer is for schools that prefer to buy by invoice.
